The v2 strategy (candidate A)

  • Coins: Bitcoin and Ethereum only. All other coins were dropped — XRP failed testing, the rest lack enough trustworthy history.
  • Buy: daily close above the highest price of the last 55 days, while above the 200-day average. Shorts mirror this in downtrends.
  • Sell: daily close below the lowest price of the last 20 days, with a disaster stop at 2.5× ATR.
  • Size: each trade risks 0.75% of the account. No leverage.
  • Most of the time it does nothing. It only trades when a real trend exists.
